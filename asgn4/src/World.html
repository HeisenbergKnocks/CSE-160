Blocky World

Please use a browser that supports "canvas"
Controls:

WASD – Move
Mouse – Look Around
Q – Pan Camera Left
E – Pan Camera Right
Scroll – Zoom Level

This is a blocky world that incorporates lighting.

You can control the light cube's Y and Z positions (the X position is already varying with a cosine function), and you can alter the color of its light using the Light Color R, G, and B sliders respectively. You can toggle the normals using the Normal On/Off buttons. You can turn the light cube's light on/off using the Light On/Off buttons, and you can turn the spotlight's light on/off using the Spot On/Off buttons.
